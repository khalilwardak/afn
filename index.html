How the numbers are made

Buy and sell prices come from each market's published board, with the time of each quote. Da Afghanistan Bank rates are read daily from the bank's own exchange-rate page, as cash and transfer rates that share the same midpoint. Market rates are read from each market's own Telegram channel.

Reference rate: the price that held longest, never an average.

  1. We record every price a market posts during the day, with the time it was posted.
  2. Buy and sell are handled separately. A repeated post of the same price does not count twice.
  3. The reference is the buy and sell price in effect longest before the 3:00 PM Kabul cutoff, then fixed and archived. Before the cutoff, the card shows the price that has held longest so far today.

Rates are indicative, not an offer to trade. Actual prices vary by exchanger, city and amount.
